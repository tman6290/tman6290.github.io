01 / Civic platform

Ikeja Corps Service Portal

Nigeria's national service office in Ikeja took attendance with a fingerprint scanner and a queue. I built a phone-first web app whose promise is on its landing page: your service year, minus the paperwork. It delivers QR check-in inside a geofence, a leave tracker that follows the real paper process, notices with push notifications, and Cooper, an AI assistant running on Google's Gemma through OpenRouter that answers only from the office's own documents.

02 / Commerce rebuild

RapJointLagos

A hip-hop book and record store in Ikoyi with a WordPress site that had survived five builds. The audit found a form plugin ten versions behind a CVSS 9.8 unauthenticated RCE, exposed admin usernames and no gateway beyond bank transfer. I patched and hardened first, then rebuilt the front end as a clean custom theme, integrated Paystack card and bank payments at checkout, and retuned the site to the client's brand book.
